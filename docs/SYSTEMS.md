## Closed loops

### The talent economy (8 of 8 hops in place)

- ✓ The academy level decides the quality of the youth intake (`js/careers.js`)
- ✓ The intake is a new player on the squad (`js/careers.js`)
- ✓ Minutes and form move a young player’s development and potential (`js/careers.js`)
- ✓ Ability and potential set his value (`js/world.js`)
- ✓ His value sets the transfer price (`js/transfers.js`)
- ✓ The fee moves both clubs’ money (`js/transfers.js`)
- ✓ Money pays for the facilities (`js/season.js`)
- ✓ The facilities set the academy and training level (`js/careers.js`)

### The results economy (7 of 7 hops in place)

- ✓ Results move the fans’ mood (`js/season.js`)
- ✓ Mood and standing set the crowd (`js/finance.js`)
- ✓ The crowd, prize money and TV set revenue (`js/finance.js`)
- ✓ Revenue sets the budget and the wage ceiling (`js/finance.js`)
- ✓ The budget limits what the club can buy (`js/transfers.js`)
- ✓ Results set the board’s confidence (`js/board.js`)
- ✓ The board’s confidence decides the manager’s job (`js/season.js`)

### The human loop (5 of 5 hops in place)

- ✓ Results and playing time move morale (`js/season.js`)
- ✓ Morale changes how well a player plays (`js/engine.js`)
- ✓ Morale and promises decide who asks to leave or to renew (`js/people.js`)
- ✓ Pressure before a match moves morale (`js/matchday.js`)
- ✓ Shouts and talks move the side in the match (`js/engine.js`)

### Club identity (4 of 6 hops in place)

- ✓ The board’s demands follow the club’s identity (`js/board.js`)
- ✓ The youth intake follows it (`js/careers.js`)
- ✓ Selling prices follow it (`js/transfers.js`)
- ✓ The budget starts from it (`js/world.js`)
- ✗ **missing:** Recruitment preferences follow it (`js/transfers.js`)
- ✗ **missing:** Fans’ style expectations follow it (`js/board.js`)

### The tactical loop (3 of 4 hops in place)

- ✓ The tactic changes what the engine does (`js/engine.js`)
- ✓ The engine counts what happened by chance type, possession and stamina (`js/engine.js`)
- ✓ The result is read back as causes (`js/matchday.js`)
- ✗ **missing:** Opponent-specific patterns shown before the match (`js/matchday.js`)
