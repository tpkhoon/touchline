# UI/UX check: 2026-10-24

Method: the `ui-ux-pro-max` rules (priority 1 to 10, the pro-rules pre-delivery checklist, and `--domain ux` searches for touch targets, focus, reduced motion and modals), the game's own rules in `CLAUDE.md`, the screen crawler (`npm run test:crawl`, phone 390×844, 78 screens), and a DOM audit in the browser at 375×812 on the five tabs, their sub-tabs, More's screens (Scout, Nations, Club) and the sheets (player, club, sim, Settings, editor). Both themes were checked for colour contrast by calculation.

Not done: a real device, a screen reader, landscape, tablet, and the largest text size.

## What passes

- **Crawler:** 78 screens, no "undefined", NaN, JavaScript error, overflow, cut-off text or invisible field (6 known problems are accepted in `tools/crawl-baseline.json`).
- **Tap targets on the tabs:** every button and control on Home, Squad, Transfers, League and More, and on the sub-tab chips, is at least 44 px. The bottom bar buttons are 73×51.
- **No sideways scroll** on any screen checked; the page is never wider than the phone.
- **Text:** body text is 13 px or more. The only 12 px lines are secondary notes; the smallest elsewhere is 10 to 11 px (below).
- **Contrast:** dark theme text 14:1 (primary), 8:1 (secondary), 5:1 (tertiary). Light theme 16:1, 7:1 and 4.7:1. Club accent colours: in the light theme all 1,877 clubs pass 4.5:1; in the dark theme the lowest is 4.0:1.
- **Safe areas, the bottom bar, the viewport meta and the reduced-motion media query** are in place. Content has 90 px of bottom padding above a 66 px bar.
- **Icons:** vector, the decorative ones are `aria-hidden`; icon buttons have `aria-label`s; stars and flags have labels; no emoji beyond nation flags.

## Findings

Severity follows the skill: High, Medium, Low.

### High

1. **Pinch zoom is switched off** (`index.html`: `user-scalable=no`). The skill's viewport rule is "never disable zoom". The game has a text size setting, which softens it, but a player who needs more than the largest size cannot zoom. Fix: remove `user-scalable=no`; if double-tap zoom misfires, add `touch-action: manipulation` to the controls.
2. **Sheets are not dialogs for assistive technology.** The sheet has no `role="dialog"`, no `aria-modal`, no label, and focus stays on the page behind it when one opens (`document.activeElement` is the body). A screen reader or keyboard user is not moved into the sheet. Fix in `UI.sheet` (`js/ui-core.js`): add the role, `aria-modal="true"` and `aria-labelledby` the title, move focus to the sheet, keep focus inside, and return it to the opener on close.
3. **Tappable cards are not reachable by keyboard.** About 19 rows on the five tabs are `div`s with `data-act` and no `role` or `tabindex` (for example the cards on Home, the rows on More and Transfers). Fix: give each `role="button"` and `tabindex="0"` and make Enter and Space activate it, in the one click-delegation handler in `js/ui-core.js`.

### Medium

4. **Selected state is colour only.** The bottom bar has no `aria-current`; sub-tab chips and segmented controls have no `aria-pressed` or `aria-selected`. A screen reader cannot tell which tab or filter is on. Fix: set `aria-current="page"` on the active tab, and `aria-pressed` on chips, from the same place that toggles the `on` class.
5. **Inputs are 15 px.** On iOS, an input under 16 px makes the browser zoom in on focus, and with zoom disabled the page then cannot be zoomed back out. All inputs and selects were 15 px. Fix: 16 px for `input, select, textarea` in `css/app.css`.
6. **Text inputs in sheets are 24 px tall.** The sim days box, the in-game editor fields and the search box measure 24 px (the selects beside them are 44). The world editor's own screens already give number fields at least 48 px (see `docs/FEATURES.md`). Fix: `min-height: 44px` on `.sheet input` (the world editor already does this on its own screens).
7. **Small tap areas inside sheets and profiles.** Inline links are 16 px high: the nation and club names on a player's sheet, the club link on a club sheet. The crest button is 26×44. The Nations rows are 199×40 and the scouting position chip "W" is 43×44. Fix: `min-height: 44px` with padding on `.tap` links in sheets, 44 px on the Nations rows, and widen the chip.
8. **No explicit focus style.** `css/app.css` sets no `:focus-visible` rule, so keyboard focus relies on the browser default, which a dark theme can hide. Fix: one rule, `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--acc); outline-offset: 2px }`.

### Low

9. **Very small text:** the tactics pitch labels (10 to 11 px, names and position lines), the radar chart labels (10.5 px), the captain tag (11 px) and the second flag (10.8 px). The rule in `CLAUDE.md` is 12 px for badges. Fix: raise to 12 px; the pitch labels may need two-line room.
10. **Dark-theme accent contrast:** for 797 of 1,877 clubs the accent used as text falls between 4.0 and 4.5:1 on the card colour. Fix: in `UI.clubAccent`, lift the lightness until it clears 4.5:1 in the dark theme, as it already does in the light one.
11. **Dismissal:** a sheet closes by the ✕ or by tapping outside; there is no Escape key and no swipe down on the grab handle, which is drawn but not draggable.
